 +-------------+
 | HOW TO PLAY |
 +-------------+
 The gameplay mechanics are already explained in the main menu if clicking on [HOW TO PLAY] so you can always go back to this.

 * GAMEPLAY:
 ------------

- [LEFT] [RIGHT] to move
- [UP] to jump
- [X] to dash
- [P] to pause the game

* DEBUG:
---------
(!) NOTE: debug needs to be enabled to use the other hotkeys.

- [SPACEBAR] to enable debug tools 
- [R] to restart the level
- [TAB] to skip to the next level
- [S] to freeze the enemies
- [D] to get dashes

 +-------------+
 | DESCRIPTION |
 +-------------+
- If your HP reaches 0 you lose, if you get to the end of level 5 you win
- Move and jump across platforms to get to the end
- Avoid the lava, slide across ice and break walls that slow you down on your way
- Collide with the bell to finish a level
- Collect coins to get dashes or hearts to get more HP
- On your way you can come across 3 different type of enemies:

	* AI Copy: copies the player's movement from a previous time
	* AI Follow: follows the player at a speed based on its distance
	* AI Patrol: goes left/right, can be jumped on to defeat it and gain a jump boost
